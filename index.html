Why Choose Us

Smart Features for Modern Billing

We've designed a system that makes water bill payment effortless for everyone in Gabiley District.

Mobile Money

Pay directly from your phone using Zaad (Telesom) or E-Dahab (Somtel) — no bank account needed.

Instant Processing

Payments are confirmed in real-time. Your account updates immediately after successful payment.

Digital Receipts

Get an SMS or email receipt instantly. No more lost paper receipts.

Payment History

View all your past payments and water usage anytime from your customer dashboard.

Due Date Reminders

Get automatic SMS reminders before your bill due date — never miss a payment.

Usage Tracking

Monitor your water consumption month by month and save money by reducing waste.

Simple Process

Pay in 3 Easy Steps

No technical skills required. Anyone can pay their water bill online.

1

Login or Register

Use your meter number as username and your password to access your account.

2

Select Payment Method

Choose Zaad or E-Dahab and enter the amount due.

3

Confirm & Get Receipt

Confirm payment on your phone and receive instant SMS receipt.
